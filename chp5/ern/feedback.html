What feedback is most useful

  • Which lane most clearly explained why a useful prototype is not enterprise-ready.
  • Where the disciplined-versus-fragile comparison still needs sharper contrast.
  • Whether replay lane changes and mastery signals helped you understand improvement across attempts.
  • Whether the facilitator presets made workshop launch and QA easier.
  • Which optional-branch step felt strongest and which still needs stronger consequence visibility.

This static build does not send feedback anywhere. Use the prompts below in a workshop, in a handoff conversation, or in the next iteration planning review.
