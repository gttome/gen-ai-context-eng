Quick start

How the mission works

Choose a scenario, review the worked example, and move through the eight operational lanes. The main path stays short. Extra support appears only when it helps you learn.

  • Core scenarios: HR, Support, and Finance are the best place to start.
  • Advanced scenarios: Executive Communications and Compliance Knowledge add more pressure and nuance after you are comfortable with the core flow.
  • Coaching style: Guided, Independent, Challenge, and Group change how much support you see without adding required steps.
  • Stakeholder reactions: show how different enterprise reviewers would respond to your choices.
  • Replay story: helps you see what changed across runs without reading a raw log.
Interpretation guide

How to read the metrics

Operational Readiness summarizes launchability. Exposure Risk is the only metric where lower is better. Trust & User Safety, Governance Completeness, Maintainability, and Rollout Confidence all improve when the workflow becomes safer, clearer, and easier to operate over time.

The goal is disciplined tradeoff management, not maximum restriction everywhere.
