# ERN Product Definition and Vision — Current Distillation

Enterprise Readiness Navigator is the flagship Chapter 5 learning studio for moving a useful AI workflow from promising prototype to governed enterprise readiness.

## Vision carried into the MVP candidate
- short, high-value enterprise readiness mission
- knowledge-worker accessible language
- coordinated readiness across privacy, policy, moderation, trust boundaries, performance, governed releases, monitoring, and fail-safe behavior
- visible tradeoffs through metrics, coaching, comparison, replay, and audit highlights
