# QA Checklist

- App loads and shows the current version pill.
- Environment pill renders correctly.
- Theme toggle works and persists after refresh.
- Scenario chips switch among all 5 scenarios.
- Evidence cards can be marked reviewed.
- Source selection unlocks progression from Evidence to Checks.
- Coach, Impact, and Playbook popups open and close cleanly.
- Fixed bottom dock remains visible while the workspace changes.
- Replay analytics appear on Summary after mission completion.
- Copy and download triage record actions work in a real browser session.
- Help and Feedback pages open and return correctly.
- No console errors appear in local and deployed runs.
