# RTC Product Definition and Vision

Reliability Triage Console is a premium guided no-code Chapter 4 learning app for knowledge workers. Its mission is to teach operational reliability triage: start from the visible symptom, anchor to the governing source, diagnose the strongest primary failure, choose the smallest credible mitigation, and preserve the lesson with a regression check.

Current iteration emphasis:
- fixed bottom coach dock
- replay analytics
- harder Chapter 4 scenarios
- mobile-first clarity over dashboard sprawl
