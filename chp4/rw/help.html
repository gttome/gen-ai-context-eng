Learning loop

Work the mission in order

  1. Read the incident and proposed change.
  2. Anchor on the baseline before judging the candidate result.
  3. Review the standing checks one by one.
  4. Use the signals and coaching text before choosing Release, Hold, or Iterate.
  5. Finish with monitoring follow-up and compare your path with the strongest-practice path.

Best practice

Start with what the baseline protects

The app is designed to teach regression protection, not surface polish. A candidate change can sound clearer while still weakening something important. The safest way to work a mission is to name what the baseline protected first, then judge whether the change preserved, improved, traded off, or weakened those protections.

External-model step

Use the optional comparison step deliberately

Some missions ask you to copy a comparison packet, run it in an external model, and paste the result back into the app. This is still an educational step. It lets you compare external output against the same standing checks and release discipline instead of trusting novelty or polish alone.

Debrief

Use the walkthrough to calibrate your judgment

The debrief is where the mission becomes reusable learning. Compare your path with the strongest-practice path, especially on the risky checks, the final decision, and the monitoring follow-up. The goal is better judgment on the next run, not just one high score.
