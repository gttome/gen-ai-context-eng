How to use Lifecycle Failure Clinic

This app teaches Chapter 2 through diagnosis, repair, review, benchmark comparison, and drift response. Choose a case, work one lifecycle phase at a time, and watch the trust chain change as you repair the system.

  • System Pulse keeps the changing metrics visible all the time.
  • Live results shows the latest preview or applied repair effect.
  • Inline Chapter Lens explains why the current phase artifact matters.
  • Phase consequence map shows artifact state, failure pressure, repair leverage, and downstream blast radius for the active phase.
  • Strongest-answer review shows the strongest diagnosis and strongest repair after you apply a repair.
  • Best full-run benchmark shows the ideal end-to-end lifecycle recovery after you finish a case.
  • Scored drift drill now includes a follow-up branch, so your first response opens a second consequence decision.
  • Replay Lab lets you save two completed runs and compare them side by side.
