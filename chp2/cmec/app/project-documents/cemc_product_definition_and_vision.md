# Source Update — Product Definition and Vision

> Continuity note: this filename is preserved for future-chat expectations. The content below reflects the current authoritative understanding for **Context Engineering Mission Control** based on the uploaded CEMC pack.

## Current authoritative interpretation
- The app exists to turn Chapter 1 into a visually rich, guided learning product.
- It should feel like a professional command center for short missions.
- The product is explicitly learner-first, not a builder workspace.
- Prepared weak packages should make cause-and-effect visible quickly.
- Visual polish, live metrics, and restrained gamification should support memorability without childishness.

## Current iteration implication
This build expresses that vision through:
- three prepared scenario families
- guided repair actions
- compare-first metrics and coaching
- manual-run support
- optional Explore More lenses

## Superseded or narrowed items
The broader long-term vision implies more breadth and polish than the current MVP. This iteration intentionally narrows scope to one strong end-to-end shell.
