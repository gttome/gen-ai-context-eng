Review, score, diagnose, change, compare, decide.

Rubric Runner Lab turns Chapter 2’s evaluation-and-iteration logic into a short, evidence-based learning loop. Score outputs against a rubric, map the dominant failure pattern, choose one targeted improvement, and compare the same cases before deciding whether to deploy, iterate, or revisit earlier lifecycle work.
