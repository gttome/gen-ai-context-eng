What this app teaches now

Rubric Runner Lab reinforces Chapter 2’s view that context engineering is a lifecycle, and that reliable outputs depend on explicit evaluation artifacts. This app focuses deeply on the chapter’s middle reliability loop: Execution → Evaluation → Iteration.

How to complete the core path

Start with the First-run walkthrough, then use the What to do now panel whenever you are unsure where to go next.

1. Choose a scenario.Each scenario is pre-authored so you can focus on judgment, not document authoring.
2. Review the artifact pack.Read the rubric, prompt version, test questions, and success signals before scoring.
3. Score every case.Use the 0-2 controls for each criterion. The lab keeps the scoring simple and fast.
4. Submit scoring and read the best-practice review.After you score first, the app reveals only the biggest score gaps and explains why a stronger evaluator would score them differently.
5. Tag the dominant failure.Choose the most important failure type per case. The lab clusters them so you can see whether the issue is isolated or recurring.
6. Pick one targeted change.This reflects the chapter’s recommendation to avoid random tweaking and make one focused adjustment where possible.
7. Compare before and after.The same test cases are reused so improvement, regression, and tradeoff behavior stay visible.
8. Read the recommendation.The app recommends deploy, iterate again, or revisit earlier phase work based on your scoring and the selected change profile.

What the six live metrics mean

Pass RateShare of cases that meet the app’s pass rule based on your rubric scores.
Criterion CoverageHow much of the rubric has actually been scored. This helps stop premature conclusions.
Failure ConcentrationHow concentrated the dominant failure pattern is across the case set.
Improvement DeltaHow much the selected change improves or harms the overall score profile.
Regression StabilityWhether the chosen change improved the same cases without hurting others.
Deploy ReadinessA recommendation state, not a decorative badge. It combines quality, stability, and governance expectations.

Optional learning checks

The core path is complete when you reach the decision summary. Optional learning checks do one of two clear things: they either raise the final deploy bar, explain why the selected fix helped, or show why skipping evaluation would be risky. They are intentionally separated so first-time learners do not feel penalized for stopping after the main lesson.
